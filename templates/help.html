Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel a booking?

Open My bookings, find the confirmed booking, and choose Cancel. Simulated hotel inventory is restored automatically.

Where can I download my itinerary?

Open My itineraries, select a trip, and choose Download PDF or Add to calendar.

Can I share a trip with someone?

Open the itinerary and create a private share link. You can revoke that link at any time.

How do I change my password?

Open Profile, choose Change password, and confirm your current password before saving the new one.

Why did my destination search fail?

Destinations need catalog data for hotel and activity recommendations. Try a supported destination such as Goa, Delhi, Jaipur, Mumbai, or Manali.
